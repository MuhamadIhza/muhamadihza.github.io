01 · Daily Word

Five letters, six shots.

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. Most days it is an everyday English word; every few rounds a light work or ops term (AUDIT, STOCK, QUOTE, SCOPE) sneaks in.

  • A Exact spot — right letter, right column.
  • U Wrong spot — in the word, different column (duplicate letters counted accurately).
  • X Not in word — no remaining match.

Saved locally in your browser (localStorage) for streak and daily progress only.
